How Does the Profit Calculator Work?

Your estimated revenue is calculated by multiplying your selling price by the number of sales.

Your estimated costs include your cost per sale, transaction fees, monthly fixed costs, and advertising expenses.

Your estimated profit is your total revenue minus your total costs. Your profit margin shows what percentage of your revenue remains after those costs.

Why Calculate Profit?

Knowing your numbers can help you understand whether a product, service, or business idea is financially sustainable before you spend more money.
