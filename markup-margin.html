What Is Markup?

Markup measures how much you increase a product's price above its cost. For example, if a product costs $20 and you sell it for $50, your markup is 150%.

Markup is calculated by comparing your profit per sale with your product cost.

What Is Profit Margin?

Profit margin shows what percentage of your selling price remains as profit after accounting for the product cost.

Markup and profit margin are related, but they are not the same thing. This is why understanding both numbers can help when setting prices.

Markup vs. Profit Margin

Suppose a product costs $20 and sells for $50. The profit is $30. The markup is 150%, while the profit margin is 60%.

Knowing the difference can help business owners avoid pricing mistakes and better understand the profitability of their products.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between markup and profit margin?

Markup compares your profit with your product cost. Profit margin compares your profit with your selling price. Because they use different bases, the percentages will usually be different.

How do I calculate markup percentage?

Markup percentage is calculated by dividing profit per sale by product cost and multiplying by 100.

How do I calculate profit margin?

Profit margin is calculated by dividing profit per sale by selling price and multiplying by 100.

Can I use this calculator for ecommerce?
