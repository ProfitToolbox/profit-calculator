How Does an E-commerce Fee Calculator Work?

An e-commerce fee calculator helps online sellers estimate how much money remains after marketplace fees, payment processing fees, product costs, shipping, advertising, and other expenses.

Start with your selling price. Then enter the fees and costs that apply to your particular business and sales channel.

Because fee structures vary between marketplaces, payment processors, stores, products, and locations, this calculator lets you enter your own fee percentages instead of assuming one universal rate.

Why Are E-commerce Fees Important?

A product can have strong sales while still producing a small profit if fees and operating costs are too high. Understanding your costs can help you evaluate pricing, advertising, shipping, and product profitability.

How Is E-commerce Profit Calculated?

Estimated profit per order is calculated by subtracting product costs, platform fees, payment processing fees, shipping, advertising, and other entered expenses from your selling price.

Profit margin is the percentage of revenue that remains after those expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What fees should an online seller include?

Common expenses include marketplace or platform fees, payment processing fees, product costs, shipping, advertising, packaging, returns, and other operating expenses.

Are e-commerce fees the same on every platform?

No. Fees can vary by platform, product category, payment method, subscription plan, location, and other factors. Check the current fee schedule for the specific platform you use.

Should I include advertising costs?

Yes, if advertising is part of your sales strategy. Including an estimated advertising cost per order can give you a more realistic picture of your profit.

What is a good e-commerce profit margin?

Profit margins vary widely depending on the product, business model, industry, pricing, and operating expenses. There is no single margin that applies to every online business.

Can I use this calculator for Amazon, Etsy, Shopify, eBay, or other stores?

Yes. Enter the applicable fee percentages and costs for your specific selling channel. Always verify the current fees directly with the platform you use.
